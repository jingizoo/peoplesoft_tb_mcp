# Month-End Close Checklist

**Owner:** Corporate Accounting · **Cadence:** Workdays 1–5 after period end

| Day | Task | Owner |
|-----|------|-------|
| WD1 | Post final AP voucher batch; run payroll journal generate | AP / Payroll |
| WD1 | Bank reconciliation for operating (1000) and payroll (1010) cash | Treasury |
| WD2 | Post revenue billing journals; reconcile AR (1100) to billing subledger | Billing |
| WD2 | Inventory (1200) reconciliation to cost subledger; post COGS | Cost Accounting |
| WD3 | Depreciation run (6500 / 1590); fixed-asset roll-forward | Fixed Assets |
| WD3 | Accruals: rent, utilities, T&E; review recurring journal list | GL |
| WD4 | Clear suspense (1999) items; document anything unresolved | Treasury |
| WD4 | Trial balance review: TB nets to zero, no unposted journals, variance scan vs prior period | GL |
| WD5 | Management P&L and balance-sheet flux commentary; close the period | Controller |

## TB review standards (WD4)

- The trial balance must net to exactly zero (debits = credits).
- No journals in status other than Posted for the closed period.
- Any account moving more than **10% and $25,000** vs prior period needs a
  one-line explanation in the flux file.
- Suspense (1999) balances must have an owner and an aging note.

## Reopening a period

Reopening requires Controller approval and a same-day notification to FP&A.
Adjustments after the audit starts go to period 998 instead — see
"Suspense Accounts and Adjustment Periods".
