# Suspense Accounts and Adjustment Periods

**Owner:** Corporate Accounting (GL team) · **Last reviewed:** 2026-03

## Suspense account policy

Account **1999 — Suspense / Unidentified Receipts** is used to park cash
receipts and other items that cannot be identified at the time of recording.

Rules:

1. Any item parked in 1999 must be **researched and cleared within 30 days**.
2. The Treasury analyst who parks an item must open a research ticket and note
   the ticket number in the journal line description.
3. A non-zero suspense balance at month end must be reported on the close
   checklist with an aging and an owner.
4. Suspense may never be used to force a journal to balance.

Escalation: items older than 60 days go to the Assistant Controller.

## Adjustment period 998

Period **998** is reserved for **year-end audit and adjusting entries** after
period 12 closes:

- Only the GL team posts to 998, with Controller approval.
- Typical entries: audit fee accruals, final tax provision true-ups, reclasses
  requested by the external auditors.
- Standard monthly reporting **excludes** 998. "Final" / "post-adjustment" /
  "audited" reporting **includes** it — say which basis you are using.

## Retained earnings

Year-end close rolls net income (all revenue and expense accounts) into
**3500 — Retained Earnings** and writes beginning balances into period 0 of the
new year. If period-0 balances don't tie to the prior year's post-adjustment
endings, stop and contact the GL team before doing any reporting.
