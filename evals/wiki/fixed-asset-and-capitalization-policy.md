# Fixed Asset and Capitalization Policy

Owner: Corporate Controller. Applies to all business units.

## Capitalization

The capitalization threshold is $10,000. Equipment, furniture and software
purchases at or above this amount are capitalized as fixed assets and
depreciated over their useful life. Purchases below the threshold are expensed
in the period incurred.

Useful life is 3 years for computer equipment, 5 years for software and
7 years for furniture and fixtures. Depreciation begins in the month the asset
is placed in service.

## Approvals

The purchase approval limit is $25,000; requisitions at or above that amount
require VP approval before the purchase order is dispatched.
