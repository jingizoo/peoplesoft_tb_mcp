# Travel and Expense Policy

Owner: Corporate Controller. Applies to all employees.

## Booking

Air travel must be booked through the corporate travel desk at least 14 days
in advance where practical. Economy class is standard for flights under six
hours; business class requires VP approval.

## Meals and lodging

Reimbursement follows the published per-diem schedule for the destination
city. Alcohol is not reimbursable. Lodging above the per-diem rate needs a
written exception from the cost-centre owner.

## Submission

Expense reports are due within 30 days of trip completion. Reports submitted
more than 60 days after the trip may be denied.
